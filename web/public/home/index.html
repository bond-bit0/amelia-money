What is Amelia Money?

Amelia Money is a small app our family uses every day. Amelia earns money for chores, schoolwork and activities. She sees what she owns, asks before she spends, and pays back a real loan on a real schedule.

We built it when the kids' money app we used shut down. We wanted something simple that we own, that keeps our data, and that we can change as she grows.

Earn

She claims a job when it's done and the money posts right away. Each job has a limit, like once every 2 days.

Own

Two numbers on her home screen: Credit is everything she owns. Available is what's left after her next loan payment.

Spend

She checks Available, asks a parent, and the parent logs the purchase. It comes out of her credit.

Borrow

A parent sets up a loan. The app builds the payment schedule, tracks late payments, and counts strikes.

Quick tasks

A parent posts a one-off task with a timer. "Walk the dog, $3, 3 hours." It disappears if nobody claims it.

Honest records

Nothing is ever deleted. A mistake gets a reversal with a note, the same way a bank does it.

Why we built it

Kids learn money by using money, not by hearing about it. An allowance handed over in cash teaches very little. A balance she earned, a purchase she had to ask for, and a loan payment due on Friday teach a lot.

Work earns money

Money shows up because she did something, and she can see exactly what.

A loan is a promise

Miss a payment and it's a strike. Three strikes and the thing the loan bought gets sold. Better to learn that at home than at 25.

Spending is a choice

Every purchase lowers a number she worked to raise. She starts asking "is it worth it?" on her own.

Business is a skill

Income from her own small business goes in next to chore money, so she sees which one grows faster.

It's part of how we homeschool. She helps set the prices and the rules, and that negotiation is a lesson too.

Get the code. It's free.

The whole app is public on GitHub. Take it, set it up for your own family, and change whatever you like. It runs on free tiers, so it costs about $0 a month.

  1. Copy the repo from GitHub.
  2. Make a free Supabase project and run the setup files.
  3. Put the site on a free host like Netlify.
  4. Create your family, invite your kids, add their jobs.

The README walks through each step. You don't need to be a programmer. We built this with an AI coding assistant, and you can use one to set it up.
